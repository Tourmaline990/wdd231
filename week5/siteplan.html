Dynamic Earth

I chose Dynamic Earth because it hints at the content of the site. 'Dynamic Earth' suggests changes, while being direct and logical

Site Purpose

The site provides information about the changes Earth is undergoing, answers technical questions, offers factual information with up-to-date data, and features a newsletter to inform users of recent developments and respond to their prompts.

Scenarios

Is Earth getting healthier or worse this year?

What natural events are happening today?

What parts of earth are changing the fastest right now?
