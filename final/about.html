Mission

The Idea

Earth is not static. It evolves, shifts, burns, freezes, and transforms. By combining NASA’s observational data with educational insights, Dynamic Earth creates an interface where science becomes exploration.

What Users Can Explore

  • Natural Events
  • Earth's Obervation
  • Space Phenomena
  • Planetary Comparison
  • Educational Insights

Vision

Dynamic Earth is designed as a growing exploratory platform. Future enhancements may include interactive visualisations, temporal comparisons, and deeper planetary datasets.
